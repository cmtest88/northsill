Northsill
Native BIM for Windows · Beta
Model once. Document from it.

The model is the easy part. The drawing set is the job.

Northsill is a BIM application for architects. You model the building once — and the drawing set is derived from it: plans, elevations, sections, schedules, sheets. Change the model and every view follows, because there is nothing to keep in step by hand.

Model

Build it once, properly.

Walls that know their layers. Doors and windows that cut them properly. Floors, roofs, stairs, railings, columns, beams and footings — modelled as what they are, not as shapes that look right in one view.

  • Layered wall types with materials and priorities
  • Hip, gable and dutch roofs; L, T and U plans
  • Levels, grids, rooms and room tags
  • Double precision throughout, on an OpenCASCADE kernel
Document

Every view comes out of the model.

The plan, the elevation and the section are computed from the one model — exactly, every time. Annotate them the way you always have, put them on sheets with your titleblock, and export the set.

  • Plans, exact hidden-line elevations and sections
  • Dimensions, tags, text and room names
  • View range, detail levels, pen standards and hatch patterns
  • Sheets with an editable titleblock → PDF, DXF, SVG, IFC
Count

The quantities are the same building.

Schedules and cut lists are counted from the model, so they are never a separate exercise and never out of date. Change the design and the numbers change with it.

  • Live schedules with grouping, filters and formatting
  • Framing cut lists, takeoffs and U-values
  • Finishes schedules from the materials you painted
Build

It knows how the building is put together.

The model carries the frame, not a pattern drawn over it. A birdsmouth exists because the roof bears on a plate and the plate is referenced to the rafter — so change the pitch and every member re-cuts itself.

  • Wall framing: studs, plates, kings, jacks, headers, sills, cripples
  • Roof framing: commons, jacks, hips, valleys, ridges, trusses
  • Derived carpentry: plumb cuts, cheek cuts, hip backing, birdsmouths
  • Masonry coursing with real units and mortar joints
Where it actually is

An honest ledger.

Northsill is in development and this page will not pretend otherwise. If you are going to put a project through it, you should know what it does not do yet.

Working today

  • Modelling, derived views, annotation, schedules and sheets
  • Export to PDF, DXF, SVG and IFC
  • Wall, roof and floor framing with cut lists
  • Undo/redo, incremental regeneration, a versioned .nsl file

Not yet

  • No IFC or DWG import — Northsill can be added to a workflow, but cannot yet join one
  • Very large models are slow to open cold (tens of thousands of elements)
  • Windows only; no macOS build
  • The installer is unsigned, so Windows will warn you
